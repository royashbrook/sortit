How to play

  1. Tap a tube to pick up what's on top.
  2. Tap another tube to drop it there.
  3. Drops only land on a matching friend, or in an empty tube.
  4. Fill a whole tube with one kind to finish it.
  5. Sort every tube to win!

Stuck? UNDO takes moves back as many times as you like, and HINT shows you a good move. Some pieces hide as a ? — move the piece on top of them to peek! Take as long as you want.
